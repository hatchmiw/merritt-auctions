## Definitions

- **EV** = expected value to the buyer *before auction fees*. For personal-use items this is condition-adjusted replacement/utility value. For resale items it is a conservative expected realizable value after normal selling friction. For intrinsic-value items it is conservative liquidation/metal value.
- **Bargain** = whether the current hammer price is attractive relative to EV and risk at this snapshot.
- **Risk-adj bid** = normal hammer-price stopping point.
- **Personal stretch** = higher hammer cap only when the user's actual use/local interest justifies it.
- **Abs ceil** = hard hammer-price ceiling. Do not exceed because of bidding momentum.

All caps are **hammer bids**, not invoice totals.

The published Merritt terms use a 13% buyer's premium, with a 3% premium discount for cash/check, plus 6% Michigan sales tax on taxable lots. Coins/currency are sales-tax exempt. These caps are deliberately conservative and generally assume the normal 13% premium/tax burden; cash/check or a valid exemption should be treated as extra margin rather than a reason to automatically raise the bid.
