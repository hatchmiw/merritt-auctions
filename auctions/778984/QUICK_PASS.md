# Quick Pass — Merritt Online Auction #49

## Read this first

This is a **catalog/title screen**, not a photo review. Its purpose is to find
the lots that justify deeper inspection without pretending a title is enough to
set a bid.

Current prices are deliberately omitted until the repository has a reliable
fresh live snapshot.

## Broad auction takeaways

- **Bulk sports-card lots, roughly 35–63:** potentially tempting at low bids,
  but major sorting/storage/time cost and much of the era can be low-value junk
  wax. Do not treat card count as value.
- **Fine glassware:** there is substantial Fenton/Northwood/Jefferson/Joe Rice/
  Zimmerman material. Some pieces may be good, but this is fragile,
  photo-dependent inventory. Selective review beats blanket bidding.
- **Coins:** probably the easiest section to price systematically because many
  lots can be bounded by metal content.
- **Michigan/local items:** the Grand Rapids Creamery crock, Detroit crock,
  Upjohn bag and Lansing ice tongs deserve extra attention.
- **Practical shop/farm items:** fewer than in the recent industrial auctions.
  The puller, measuring tools, charger/power item, heater, tarps and toolbox are
  the clearest utility candidates.

## Things not to chase from title alone

- Common glass merely because it is named Fenton/Northwood.
- Huge sports-card lots based on quantity alone.
- Common cameras, binoculars or small electronics without model/condition confirmation.
- Claimed precious-metal or rare collectible items when photos do not verify identifying marks.
