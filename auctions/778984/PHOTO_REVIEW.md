## Critical coin correction

The catalog descriptions for **lots 168–177 and 179** say “40% Silver,” but the photographed coins do **not** support that description.

- **168:** 1977-D Eisenhower dollar — ordinary copper-nickel clad issue.
- **169:** 1978 Eisenhower dollar — no silver-issue evidence in the photo; ordinary clad.
- **170:** 1978 Eisenhower dollar — ordinary clad.
- **171:** 1776–1976-D Eisenhower dollar — D mint is visible; ordinary clad, not the 40% silver collector issue.
- **172–176:** 1974/1973/1972 coins shown without the San Francisco silver-issue characteristics; treat as ordinary clad.
- **177:** 1971 Eisenhower dollar — no silver-issue evidence; treat as clad.
- **179:** 1972 Eisenhower dollar — ordinary clad-looking coin with a dark corrosion/spot issue.

**Do not bid on lots 168–177 or 179 as silver bullion.**

Additional coin findings:

- **163m–167m:** photographed as 1 oz American Silver Eagles (2025/2025/2015/2004/2025) in capsules. Photos are consistent with the catalog identity, but photos alone cannot authenticate or grade bullion coins.
- **178:** 1928 Peace dollar is a 90% silver type, but this example is heavily worn and has a severely battered/distorted rim. Treat primarily as low-grade/melt-oriented rather than a premium numismatic coin.
- **180:** one **1964 Kennedy half** (90% silver) plus one **2001 Kennedy half** (clad). Only the 1964 half carries silver melt value.
